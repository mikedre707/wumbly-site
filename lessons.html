The lessons

Two ladders your child climbs at their own pace. Every lesson is 10 questions, freshly generated every time (no repeated quizzes), read aloud by Wumbly, and safe to leave and pick back up mid-way. You can try every one of these right now — in your browser.

📚 Reading

Reading Start

Listen and tap — no reading needed

For pre-readers: Wumbly reads a tiny story out loud and your child answers by tapping pictures. Comprehension before decoding, for ages 3 and up.

Try it →
Reading 1

First letters, sounds, and tiny words

The starter letters (s, a, t, p, i, n, m), what each one says, blending sounds into first words like "sat" and "pin", and reading them out loud.

Try it →
Reading 2

More letters, short words, first stories

The rest of the everyday alphabet, building and spelling short words, first full sentences, and little passages with a question to answer.

Try it →
Reading 3

Tricky sounds like sh, ch, and th

The two-letter teams that make one sound — sh, ch, th, qu — inside words, sentences, and stories, plus the trickier letters j, k, v, x, y, z.

Try it →
Reading 4

Letter teams, blends, and real sentences

Consonant blends like st, fr, and mp, longer sentences read aloud, and real short passages with comprehension questions.

Try it →
Reading 5

Big words and longer stories

Two-syllable and big, bold words, confident reading out loud, and the longest stories with real comprehension work.
