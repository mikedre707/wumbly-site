What's inside

📚

Reading 1–5

A real phonics ladder — from first letter sounds and tiny words to tricky sounds, blends, and big-kid stories. Pick a level, or let the demo show the way.

🔢

Math Start–5

From counting cookies all the way to 3-digit sums and times-and-sharing. Ten fresh questions every time — never the same quiz twice.

✨

Story time

Every reading lesson ends with a brand-new story written just for your child, starring the exact words they just practiced. Reading practice disguised as a treat.

🎙️

Talks and listens

Wumbly reads every question out loud, and kids can read back to the microphone. Their voice is understood right on the device — it never leaves the phone.

🖐️

Made for little hands

Big candy buttons, gentle hints instead of red X's, an exit that's always there, and a "Keep going" that remembers the exact question they left off on.

👨‍👩‍👧

Grown-ups in control

Up to four kid profiles, a parent corner behind a grown-ups-only gate, and a friendly weekly summary of what each child practiced and mastered.
