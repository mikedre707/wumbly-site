Deleting your data

Effective September 15, 2026

The short version: Wumbly has no accounts, and your child's profiles, progress, stars, and stickers live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that data. The one thing that exists outside your device — an anonymous purchase record — can be wiped by emailing us.

Data on your device (deleted by you, instantly)

Everything Wumbly knows about your child — profiles, lesson progress, streaks, stickers, and game best scores — is stored only on the phone or tablet where it was created. It is never uploaded anywhere.

  • To delete one child's profile: open the Grown-ups area in the app and delete the profile.
  • To delete everything: uninstall the app. No copy exists anywhere else.

Purchase records (deleted on request)

If you subscribed to Wumbly Pro, our billing provider (RevenueCat) holds an anonymous record — a random ID and the purchase history — so your subscription keeps working. It contains no name, email, or child data.

To have it deleted, email mike@profsearch.net with the subject "Wumbly data deletion request." We will delete the record within 30 days and confirm by reply. Note that deleting it does not cancel a subscription — manage that in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

What we never had

There is nothing else to delete: Wumbly has no accounts, collects no names or emails, shows no ads, and the microphone's read-aloud checking happens entirely on the device — what your child says is never recorded or transmitted.

Questions? mike@profsearch.net — see also our Privacy Policy.
